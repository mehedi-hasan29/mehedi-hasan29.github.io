M.S. Thesis

Title: “Local-Excitation Interference, Not Donor–Acceptor Distance, Controls the Radiative Strength of a Through-Space Charge-Transfer TADF Emitter”

Abstract:

This thesis asks if exchange coupling and radiative strength respond alike. The test case is STF-DBTS, a through-space charge-transfer TADF emitter. Calculations use ωB97X-D/def2-SVP with continuum toluene solvation. Local triplets split the triplet charge-transfer configuration over twelve states. Only a diabatic gap measures the exchange splitting, +50.6 meV. It is built alike on both spin manifolds. Along the fluorene-acceptor torsion, the exchange integral varies two-fold. It tracks HOMO-LUMO overlap (r = 0.92). Interference with a small acridine-local excitation sets the S1 transition dipole. At one conformer, the gap falls 19% but the dipole falls 45%. These results point to overlap and local-excitation admixture as design variables.
