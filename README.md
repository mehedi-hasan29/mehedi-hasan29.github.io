## MD. Mehedi Hasan Moon

`MS Physics · Organic Optoelectronics Lab · SUST, Sylhet`

Three quarters of the excitons in an OLED are triplets, and in a conventional emitter they are dark. I design **π-stacked donor–acceptor triads** for thermally activated delayed fluorescence — rigid, U-shaped geometries that clamp donor and acceptor face to face so charge transfer happens *through space* rather than along a conjugated bridge. Separating the frontier orbitals shrinks the exchange integral; once ΔE<sub>ST</sub> drops below ~0.1 eV, ambient thermal energy alone lifts those triplets back into the emissive channel.

### Three threads

**i · Through-space charge transfer** — rigid U-shaped triads that hold donor and acceptor cofacial, designing away the orbital overlap that normally enforces a large exchange splitting.

**ii · Gap engineering for RISC** — driving ΔE<sub>ST</sub> below 0.1 eV with dispersion-corrected DFT and TD-DFT, to blunt efficiency roll-off at high current density.

**iii · Neutrinos from neutron capture** — coupling SkyNet reaction networks to MESA stellar models so neutrino emission through neutron-capture burning is a time-resolved output rather than a post-hoc estimate.
